Official sources

Prefer Google Play, Apple App Store, or developer websites. Verify developer names, domains, update records, and privacy labels.

Permissions

Review location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, accessibility, file access, and background activity before installing.

Account safety

For finance, wallet, crypto, cloud drive, and chat apps, enable two-factor authentication and login alerts after installation.
