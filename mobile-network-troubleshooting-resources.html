Quick troubleshooting sequence

  • Confirm account status, plan eligibility, SIM/eSIM activation and carrier coverage before changing advanced settings.
  • Check airplane mode, preferred network type, data roaming, APN profile, VPN/private DNS and dual-SIM default data selection.
  • Test another location or device when possible to separate phone-side issues from carrier-side or coverage-side issues.
  • For travel roaming, confirm country/region support, roaming package activation, APN requirements and operator selection rules.
