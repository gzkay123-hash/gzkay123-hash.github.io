Quick safety checklist

  • Prefer the publisher's official website, Google Play, Apple App Store, Microsoft Store or another clearly documented official channel.
  • Check the developer name, package/app identity, recent update history, permissions and user reports before installation.
  • Be careful with repackaged APK files, modded clients, cracked versions, fake update prompts and aggressive permission requests.
  • For banking, crypto, wallet, messaging and account-security apps, avoid unofficial mirrors unless the publisher explicitly documents them.
